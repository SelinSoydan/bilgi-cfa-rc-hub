Where do we start?

Not with the DCF. A valuation is a story told in numbers, so we learn the story first. The method comes last, because the business decides the method.

The story

Read the latest investor presentation, then the first part of the 2025 Annual Report.

Done when each of us can say in three sentences what Borusan sells, who buys it, and where the money comes from.

The numbers

Open the financial reports for 2023, 2024, 2025 and the latest quarter. Build one table in US dollars: sales volume, revenue, EBITDA, margin, debt, cash flow.

Why dollars? The company gives its own guidance in dollars, so our numbers can be compared with it directly.

The industry

Who are the peers, and what moves demand? Borusan sells into four areas: infrastructure and projects, industrial and construction, automotive and industrial uses, and energy.

Ask why Borusan's margin is higher or lower than its peers: price, volume, cost, or location?

The method

Only now. Our main tool is a DCF of the whole firm (free cash flow to the firm, discounted at WACC), checked against peer multiples like EV/EBITDA.

Why this pair? The DCF shows what the business is worth from its own cash. Multiples show what the market pays for similar businesses. If the two disagree a lot, one of our assumptions is wrong.

What CFA wants to see

Page 1

A clear call

Buy, Hold or Sell, a target price, and three reasons. Each reason carries a number from later pages.

Forecast

Drivers, not guesses

Revenue = volume × price. Margin comes from where we are in the steel cycle. Every forecast line has a reason.

Valuation

Know your key assumption

Judges always ask which assumption matters most. Show a sensitivity table for growth and discount rate.

Risks

Probability and impact

A risk matrix, and what each big risk does to our target price.

ESG

Only what changes value

For a steel pipe maker: carbon costs (CBAM), energy use, safety. Not a checklist.

Honesty

Sources and AI

Public information only, every number with a source, every AI use written in our log.

Example companies to learn from

Closest twin

Tenaris

A global steel pipe maker. In its 2025 annual filing, North America is its largest region for pipe sales, close to half. Like Borusan, much of its business depends on US energy and US tariffs.

Learn from it: how a pipe maker explains itself to investors: rig count, pipe prices, order backlog, tariffs. Check the exact share in the filing before we quote it.

Tenaris Form 20-F 2025 (SEC)

Winning student team

U.S. Steel

A University of Pittsburgh team won the 2020-21 CFA Society Pittsburgh challenge on U.S. Steel. They used Porter's Five Forces, a SWOT, and a Monte Carlo study of how steel prices change the value.

Learn from it: for a steel company, show the judges how the price cycle moves the value, not one single number.

How we assign work
